About Us

Crustify started as a single stone-baked oven and a stubborn belief that fast food deserves the same care as a slow kitchen.

Today we serve pizza, burgers, sandwiches, shawarma, sides and more — all cooked to order and delivered while they're still hot. No heat lamps, no shortcuts.

🍕 Our Story

Founded by a group of friends who were tired of lukewarm deliveries, Crustify began with one oven, one van and a lot of garlic.

🌿 Fresh Ingredients

We buy produce daily and prove our dough for 24 hours before it ever meets the oven. Nothing is frozen, nothing sits.

🔥 Our Passion

Our cooks are paid to be good at this, not fast. Every dish is made to order, right after you press the button.

Test, hygiene and happiness

The three things we will never compromise on.
